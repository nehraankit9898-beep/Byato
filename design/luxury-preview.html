Why it reads expensive

Five changes. No new features.

Luxury is a set of small refusals: refuse saturation, refuse heavy shadows, refuse three accents, refuse fast motion.

Design system

Tokens, type, rules.

Everything lives in globals.css as CSS variables — components inherit the new mood without being rewritten.

01 · Palette

Ivory, ink & one gold

Warm paper instead of pure white; espresso instead of black; champagne only for rules, eyebrows and focus.

INK · GRAPHITE · STONE · CHAMPAGNE · GOLD WASH · IVORY

02 · Typography

Cormorant + Geist

Good evening
Guest intelligence, quietly kept
Functional text stays in Geist — labels, tables, inputs, buttons. Serif is reserved for headlines, KPI numerals and the brand.
03 · Rules

Restraint, codified

  • One accent per screen. Gold never fills a large area.
  • Hairlines instead of shadows for structure.
  • 6px radii. Buttons and cards share the same corner.
  • 420ms easing — nothing snaps.
  • 8pt spacing scale; generous 108px section rhythm.
